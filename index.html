Research

Severe maternal morbidity, the unexpected outcomes of labor and delivery that threaten a mother's life or health, is rare in any one hospital and easy to miss until it happens. My research, advised by Dr. Kazem Taghva, asks how much of that risk is already written in the claims a patient generates during pregnancy, and how to put the answer in front of the nurses and physicians who can act on it.

The work uses the Virginia All-Payer Claims Database, which covers deliveries from 2018 to 2022 across Medicaid, Medicare Advantage, and commercial plans. Because the data are protected, every model, including the language models, runs on local hardware under a data-use agreement.

  1. Claims59M
    Raw medical and enrollment claims merged and validated in a reproducible ETL pipeline.
  2. Pregnancies325k
    Pregnancy episodes reconstructed for about 397k patients, with prenatal and postpartum windows labeled.
  3. Signals26 · 21
    Prenatal risk factors, kept separate from the CDC severe maternal morbidity indicators.
  4. ModelsAUPRC
    Ordinal and survival models, Transformers, boosting and graph networks, explained with SHAP and LIME.
  5. AnswersLLM + RAG
    A fine-tuned local model that narrates episodes and answers clinical questions.

Selected projects

  • Planned

    Clinical Q&A assistant with RAG In design

    A nurse asks how a prenatal ICD-10 code in a patient's record relates to severe maternal morbidity. The assistant retrieves the matching mined rules and clinical risk-factor evidence, then answers in plain language with the fine-tuned local LLM. Evaluation will pair clinical experts with LLM-as-a-judge. Everything runs on-premise.

    RAG · embedding retrieval · fine-tuned 8B LLM · association rules

  • 2026

    Claims to clinical narratives In progress

    Turns a pregnancy episode into a week-by-week English summary. Codes become a JSON timeline, then template text, then a fluent rewrite by a local open-weight model. Those pairs fine-tune an 8B clinical model. A 70B candidate was rejected for adding interpretation the data did not support.

    LLM fine-tuning · data-to-text · faithfulness testing · on-prem GPU

  • 2026

    Preterm birth by gestational-age band In progress

    269,070 live births, 30,807 preterm (11.4%), split into WHO bands and spontaneous vs. indicated subtypes. Risk factors and SMM are profiled per band, and five model families are compared under a leakage-controlled landmark design.

    ordinal regression · competing-risks survival · sequence Transformer · FT-Transformer · EBM

  • 2026

    From prenatal codes to SMM

    Spark FP-Growth and Apriori over 6M ICD-10 records from 291,761 pregnancies produced 5,666 directional rules from prenatal diagnoses to SMM indicators, each explained in plain language by a local LLM. These rules are the knowledge base for the Q&A assistant.

    PySpark · association-rule mining · Llama 3.2

  • 2025–26

    Explainable pregnancy-loss prediction

    Birth vs. loss on 241,076 episodes, comparing a 12,946-feature bag-of-codes model, an interpretable 285-feature model, and sentence embeddings. Thresholds were calibrated for about 0.90 loss recall. SHAP and LIME agreed on 72.7% of feature-level comparisons in the analysis of missed losses.

    XGBoost · Bayesian classifiers · DistilBERT · MiniLM · SHAP · LIME

  • 2025

    AI pregnancy episode identification

    A two-stage Transformer and rule-based framework that finds and numbers prenatal, delivery and postpartum episodes for 397k patients, with more than 93% of patients' pregnancies labeled perfectly.

    Transformers · rule engine · MariaDB ETL at 59M claims

  • 2024

    LLM annotation and data linking

    Built a biomedical NER dataset from ClinicalTrials.gov using LLMs, and at the School of Public Policy linked noisy global emissions records with fuzzy matching, GPT and BERT classifiers.

    GPT · BERT · NER · fuzzy matching
